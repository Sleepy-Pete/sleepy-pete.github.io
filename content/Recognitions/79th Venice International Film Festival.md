**Immersive Media - Reimagined Vol. I: Nyssa**

## Recognition

Reimagined Vol. I: Nyssa was selected for the Immersive Media section of the 79th Venice International Film Festival in 2022.

**Venice Immersive 2022**: [Reimagined Vol. I: Nyssa](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2022/venice-immersive/reimagined-volume-i-nyssa)

## About Reimagined Vol. I: Nyssa

Reimagined Vol. I: Nyssa is a 15-minute VR animated short film that reimagines the Brothers Grimm folktale. Created entirely in Quill, this project demonstrates the artistic potential of VR as a primary production medium for narrative storytelling.

**Director:** Julie Cavaliere  
**Studio:** Studio Syro  
**Platform:** Meta Quest TV

## About Venice International Film Festival

The Venice International Film Festival is one of the most prestigious and publicized film festivals in the world. Held annually in Venice, Italy, it showcases innovative and work in cinema and immersive media.
