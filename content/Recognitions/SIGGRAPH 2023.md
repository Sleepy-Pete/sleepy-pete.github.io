**VR Theater Official Selection**
**Reimagined Volume I: Nyssa**

## Recognition

Reimagined Volume I: Nyssa was selected for the VR Theater at ACM SIGGRAPH 2023 in Los Angeles. The VR Theater is SIGGRAPH's juried program of immersive storytelling.

**ACM Digital Library**: [Reimagined Volume I: Nyssa, ACM SIGGRAPH 2023 VR Theater](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3577025.3580395)

## About Nyssa

[[Productions/Studio Syro/Animated Experiences/Reimagined Volume I - Nyssa|Reimagined Volume I: Nyssa]] is a 15-minute VR animated short film that reimagines the Brothers Grimm folktale "A Tale of the Boy Who Went Forth to Learn What Fear Was." It was written and directed by Julie Cavaliere, hand-painted in Quill by Studio Syro, and released on Meta Quest TV in March 2023.

**Director:** Julie Cavaliere (Very Cavaliere Productions)
**Studio:** Studio Syro
**Platform:** Meta Quest TV
