## Overview

Tales From Soda Island sends you on a journey through the musical ecosystem of Soda Island. You discover strange creatures, ancient rituals, and cosmic symphonies working together in harmony.

The series is an anthology of events that unfold on Soda Island. Each story is unique and follows its own theme, with a central narrative woven through the whole series. It was created entirely in VR using Quill by Smoothstep over three years. The seven episodes run over two hours in total and have 5 million+ views on the Meta Quest platform.

## My role

I ran the project from pitch to release. That meant working with the director through every stage, from ideation, scriptwriting, storyboards, and animatics to rough cuts, alpha, and final delivery, and keeping executive producers, artists, animators, audio directors, and sound designers aligned along the way.

I managed the schedule, the task tracking, and all communication with Meta as funder and publisher, including contracts, milestones, and invoicing. I built and hired the team and handled budgeting, payroll, and legal review.

On the technical side, I owned spatial audio for every episode, provided IT support through production, and spent R&D time improving the Quill-to-engine pipeline and the shaders the series shipped on.
